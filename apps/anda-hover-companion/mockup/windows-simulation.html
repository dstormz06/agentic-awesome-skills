Look and feel
APPEARANCE · FULL CARD
APPEARANCE · COMPACT BOX

Compact boxes are small enough to leave an open email readable. They make no AI call and never take keyboard focus: click one, or press the hotkey again on the same number, to open its full card. All looks share identical anatomy and meaning. This is preference, never information.

CARD TRANSPARENCY
90%

100% is solid; 40% lets what is behind the card show through. The card starts at 90%. Only the card surface becomes translucent (a bottom sheet on a phone is always solid). Text and status colors stay fully opaque, but on a busy background they are easier to read at a higher value.

LOOKUP WINDOW

One Bar suggests what to open from what you type and what you selected. Every suggestion is a row you press; nothing opens by itself.

AUTO-HIDE

Hides a card or box after this long with no mouse or key activity. Never while your pointer is over it, while it is pinned, or while the drawer is open.

How it starts
CONSENT MODEL

Still starts only from your own selection and shortcut. A peek makes no AI call. Off: the invite always waits for your click.

SELECT & PEEK

Off: opens the lookup box instead and no keystroke is ever sent. Terminals are always skipped, because Ctrl+C there interrupts a running command.

SHORTCUTS · CLICK A BOX, THEN PRESS THE KEYS
If a shortcut does nothing here, your computer or another program probably uses it before this page can see it (many apps claim a global shortcut). Pick another below, or use the on-screen buttons or the tray icon, which always work.

Click a box and press the new shortcut; Tab or Escape leaves the box without changing it. Please use at least two modifiers, one of them Ctrl or Alt, so everyday shortcuts like Ctrl+C keep working. On keyboards where Ctrl+Alt is AltGr, choose Ctrl+Shift instead. Never the Windows key.

DISPLAY · TEXT SIZE AND SPACING

Text size applies to the card and to this app's windows; spacing applies to the card. Neither changes other programs.

RECENT ANDAS

Only six-digit numbers are kept, in this PC's settings, and never sent anywhere. Turning this off clears them. ANDAs you keep with “Keep in my list” on a card stay until you remove them and are saved in a backup.

WATCH MY BACK · SHORT NOTICES NEAR THE CLOCK

Both are off until you switch them on. A notice is one short line near the clock. It never takes the keyboard, hides by itself, and shows the ANDA number and a short reason, never your notes. The Windows app would check when it starts, when your portfolio file changes and once each morning; this page checks when you switch a box on, when the demo date changes and when you press Check now. Division news is plain text from one Word document: anyone who can edit that document can put a line on your screen, so each line is cut to 240 characters and counts toward the daily limit. A notice fires only on what the data shows; silence does not mean all is well.

OTHER WAYS TO START IT

Off by default. In the Windows app it uses a mouse hook that sees only the middle button, never the pointer position or any text, and the click still reaches the program under it. Some security software flags mouse hooks. A plain middle-click scrolls or opens links in many programs, so Ctrl is recommended. Here it works on the text in the windows below; the page only stops the browser's own scroll mode. The shortcuts and the tray icon always work.

Weekends and federal holidays are always flagged (U.S. federal calendar, OPM). This only shows you the date; it never says how a goal date rule applies.

My files and writing
MY DATA · YOUR OWN FILES, READ-ONLY, ON THIS PC
Simulated here. In the Windows app, Choose… opens a file picker and the file stays on your PC and is only ever read. Nothing is read or sent from this page, so do not use real files with it.

Update now fetches that one address, only when you press it, and keeps the file only if it really is a Drugs@FDA data file. It needs the network and is the only thing here that does. The PSG line has no official source connected: it shows a date from a PSG column in your own sheet, or says it is not available.

ANDA PAGE · WHERE “OPEN ANDA PAGE” ON THE CARD GOES

Write {anda} where the six-digit number goes. Only an https address is opened, in your default browser, and only the number is put into it. Leave it empty and the button says so instead of guessing. The address shown at first is an example for this simulation.

CARD DETAILS · COLUMNS OF YOUR SHEET SHOWN WHEN A CARD IS EXPANDED

Each is a column of your portfolio file, shown with a Copy button when you expand a card (E). A column your sheet does not have reads “not in your sheet”. Nothing here is looked up on the web.

MY WRITING · TONE, STYLE, SIGN-OFF, QUICK REPLIES

Tone, style and sign-off steer Proofread, Rewrite and Fact-check (only once a model is approved). They can never override the fixed rules: no changed numbers or dates, no invented facts. Quick replies are templates filled from the card (no AI): the Quick replies button on a card copies one with its numbers and dates filled in.

My to-do list
MY TO-DO LIST · YOUR DAILY WORKLOAD, ANDA RELATED OR NOT
Notes to my to-do list

Click the box, then press the keys. Select rough notes anywhere (an email, a chat message, or rough notes), press this shortcut, and they become suggested to-do items. None means no shortcut: the tray menu still opens it.

WHEN I PRESS IT

Show me the items first lets you untick, change or rename each one before anything is added. Add them at once puts every item that looks like a task on your to-do list straight away, with an Undo button for that batch.

OPEN IT · KEEP IT TIDY

This is your own workload list, for any task, ANDA related or not. It is apart from the ANDA to-do list, which is built from your ANDA data: it has its own shortcut, window and file (mytodo.json on this PC in the Windows app; here it lives only on this page). Notes are read by simple rules (no AI, nothing is sent anywhere), and the reading never invents a day: what it did not find stays empty. Items that repeat come back by themselves.

My actions, agents and ANDA to-do list
MY ACTIONS · YOUR OWN PROMPT, AGENT OR TO-DO ITEM, ON YOUR OWN SHORTCUT
Simulated here. Nothing is sent from this page: an AI or agent reply below is a labeled stand-in, never a real answer.

Select a number anywhere, then press the shortcut you chose for an action. Add to my to-do list puts it on your list. Fill my prompt shows your own prompt with the card's facts filled in, ready to copy; no AI is called. Ask my AI and Run an agent show exactly what would be sent, and send only after you press Send (unless you turn that off for that one action). Every action is also in the tray menu.

MY AGENTS · ALERT HUB, PATENT ANALYSIS, TRIAGING AGENT AND YOUR OWN

An agent is any service you can reach with an address. Alert Hub, Patent Analysis and Triaging Agent are listed so they can be set up: this app does not know their addresses or message format and does not guess them, so each stays off until you enter the details your IT team gives you. Nothing is sent for an agent that is off. The token stays in the environment variable you name and is never saved by this app.

An agent is asked only when you press Run on a card or use its action, unless you set it to run by itself. By itself means: when a full card opens, or only when the ANDA is at risk. A peek never asks an agent. Its answer appears in the card's drawer as the agent's own words, and nothing joins your to-do list until you press its button.

MY ANDA TO-DO LIST · LIVE, FROM YOUR ITEMS AND YOUR DATA
While the list is open, refresh every

The list updates itself only while it is open: at the interval you chose, and at once when you change the list. A changed portfolio file is picked up at the next refresh. When it is closed nothing runs. In the Windows app it is kept in a plain file on this PC (todo.json) that you can read, and is never sent anywhere; here it lives only in this page's memory. Items from your data follow the same rules as the card, so the two cannot disagree; ticking one off keeps it done until the date or the situation changes.

AI and connections
COMPANION LENS · PROMPT LIBRARY

Your own stored prompt steers the brief's emphasis. A lens can never override the facts taken from the record.

CONNECTION · WHICH API, IF ANY
My API: the address, header and body of my own service

Nothing here is a secret: the token stays in the environment variable you name and is never saved by the app. Only https:// addresses are used (http:// for localhost). The body shown is only an example of one common shape; use the shape your own service documents. A reply that does not have text at the path is reported without showing what came back. In this simulation the form is checked but nothing is ever sent.

Auto uses an AI access key (ANTHROPIC_API_KEY) or the agency AI service (Elsa) set up on this PC, if one exists, and is otherwise offline. Offline never sends anything, and the app is fully functional offline. The banner at the top says Connected only after a request has succeeded. This page cannot make requests, so it never shows Connected.

OUTLOOK · OPTIONAL, READ-ONLY
Rehearsal only. This page never contacts Microsoft and has no mailbox. Sign in shows the steps with a made-up code, and the card shows made-up messages.

Off by default. When on, an open card can list the latest messages that mention its six-digit number: sender, subject, date and a short preview. It is read-only and searches only for that number.

You sign in on Microsoft's own page, so the app never sees your password. The sign-in stays in memory and ends when the app closes. Mail is never saved and never sent to an AI service.

Your organization must register an app and allow mail access. Please ask your IT service desk for its Application (client) ID. Written from Microsoft's public documentation and tested against a stand-in server only, not against Microsoft.

Maintenance
BACK UP, RESTORE, RESET
Backup as text: copy it, or paste one to restore

A backup holds your settings, lenses and quick replies, so a new PC or a colleague can start from yours. It never holds a secret or a file location. In this simulation your browser may block the file download; the text box above gives the same backup, so it can be copied and pasted back. The Windows app asks where to save a file.

WHICH TOOLS ARE SHOWN

Off shows the three main tools: look up an ANDA, review a draft and ask about a selection. A few small extras, such as the Watch list and Quick replies, stay in the tray menu, and the looks are always under Look and feel. Turning this on loses nothing and changes no data.

THIS WINDOW · ARRANGES ITSELF

Sections open when something in them needs attention (a shortcut another program owns, a file that cannot be read, a connection that was not tested), when you are just starting, and when you open them often. The only thing kept is a count of how often you open each section (in the Windows app, in settings.json on this PC; in this simulation, only on this page). No text, no ANDA numbers, nothing is sent anywhere. Turn this off and no more counts are kept.
