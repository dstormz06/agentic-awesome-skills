ANDA COMPANION (NOTULA): LIVE DEMO
Thank you for trying it. Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. Fictional data only. A prototype, not an FDA product.

BEFORE YOU START
If your organization manages this computer, please do not run the demo until your IT or
security team has approved it. It is an unsigned program that sends keystrokes and reads
the clipboard. Use the browser simulation or the slides instead.

SET UP (about 2 minutes)
  1. Right-click the downloaded .zip and choose Extract All. Do not run it from inside the zip.
  2. Double-click Start-Demo.bat.
  3. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", that is expected: the demo is not
     code-signed. If your organization allows it, choose More info, then Run anyway.
  4. A Settings window and a sample card open, and a sample memo opens in Notepad.
  5. Find the tray icon near the clock. Windows 11 hides new icons under the ^ arrow.
     Drag the icon onto the taskbar to keep it visible.

TRY IT
  1. In Notepad, double-click a number such as 216482 and press Ctrl+Alt+A.
     A new install shows a small invite that you click. An at-risk ANDA such as 216482
     opens a small peek by itself (Settings can turn that off). Click "Full brief" to
     see the whole card, including the WATCH line about weekend and holiday dates.
  2. Select any text and press Ctrl+Alt+Shift+R. It reviews a draft against the record and against itself
     (dates, weekdays, holidays, repeated words, a placeholder left in). No AI is used and nothing
     leaves the computer.
  3. Press Ctrl+Alt+Space from anywhere and type a number.
  4. In Notepad, select the spreadsheet cells near the bottom of the memo and press Ctrl+Alt+Shift+P (Ask).
     It says what you selected and suggests one button. Quick checks (duplicates, blanks, totals, compare
     two columns) run on this PC with no AI. A prompt of your own is filled in and shown to you first.
  5. Open the tray icon's Watch list: every ANDA with something worth a look, worst first.
  6. Read the card's PSG and RLD LABEL lines (a date, or a brief reason there is none) and
     the pill that says whether an API is connected. Offline is the default.
  7. Press the Quick replies button (or R) on a card to copy one of your own templates, filled in.

MAKE IT YOURS (tray icon, then Settings)
  The app opens focused: Lookup, Review a draft and Ask. Settings opens on what needs a look and keeps
  the other sections collapsed; click a heading to open one, or use "Expand all". A "Go to" list at the
  top jumps to any section. Nothing here needs a rebuild.
  Tick Maintenance, "Show all tools" to also see the two to-do lists, agents and dates. The looks are
  always under "Look and feel". Nothing is deleted, and what you chose comes back. Items below
  marked (all tools) appear only then.
  - Appearance: 20 looks (T is the default; L to O are small boxes that stay out of the way
    of an email), auto-hide (10 seconds to start), transparency (40 to 100 percent, starting at 90), how a card opens.
  - Shortcuts: your own keys, with a live note on any that another program already uses.
    An optional middle-click is off by default.
  - My data: your Excel or CSV portfolio, notes folder and Drugs@FDA file. They are only read.
  - My writing: tone, style, sign-off and quick replies.
  - Ask: my stored prompts (called My actions when all tools are shown): your own prompt or AI request, used by
    Ask or on a shortcut you choose. Anything that goes to an AI or an agent shows you the exact text first.
  - (all tools) My agents: Alert Hub, Patent Analysis and Triaging Agent are listed but switched off, because
    their addresses are not known here. Your IT team supplies them. Your own agents work the same way.
  - (all tools) ANDA to-do list: the tray icon's "ANDA to-do list..." shows your items and what your data says
    needs a look. It updates itself only while it is open.
  - (all tools) My to-do list: a separate list for your daily workload (ANDA related or not). Select rough notes anywhere and
    press Ctrl+Alt+Shift+L (or choose another shortcut here): they become suggested items that you can
    change before you press Add. They are read by simple rules on this PC, with no AI.
  - Connection: Auto, Offline, Claude (with its model name) or your own API. The banner says
    "Connected" only after a request has really succeeded.
  - Outlook (optional, off by default): read-only mail about the open ANDA.
  - Back up, restore or reset your settings.
  - Tray icon, then Prompt library: add your own lens.

THE NEWER DESIGN
  This build has the Loupe icon and the default card that leads with one answer and one main
  action (Copy status, or Enter). One Bar, Today, recent ANDAs, card details with Copy, notices
  near the clock and the one question before sending are shown in the browser simulation only.
  TRIAL-SHEET.md lists five everyday tasks for judging whether the design helps.

IF SOMETHING DOES NOT WORK
  - Nothing appears when you press Ctrl+Alt+A: another program may own that combination.
    The tray menu shows which shortcuts are live. Ctrl+Alt+Space and the tray icon always work.
  - Blocked by your computer's security software: that is your organization's policy at
    work. Please use the browser simulation instead. It installs nothing.
  - To check the app yourself, open a Command Prompt in this folder and run:
        AndaCompanion.exe --selftest report
    It takes about a minute and writes report\report.md with what works on your machine,
    plus pictures. Please do not type or click while it runs, because it presses the shortcuts
    itself. Your clipboard is put back afterwards.

EDGE EXTENSION (optional, separate)
  See extension\README.md in the repository. It adds a right-click menu in Edge and can use
  this app's data. Please do not add it to a managed computer before IT approves it.

CHECK THE DOWNLOAD
  SHA256SUMS.txt lists the expected hash of AndaCompanion.exe. In PowerShell:
        Get-FileHash .\AndaCompanion.exe -Algorithm SHA256

REMOVE IT
  Choose Exit from the tray icon, then delete this folder. Nothing was installed, and nothing
  was written outside the "data" folder.

NO AI ACCESS IS NEEDED
  The app works fully offline. Generated briefs appear only after API access is approved and
  chosen in Settings.
